A native Linux game launcher. The mark is a lattice of eight petals around an open centre, glazed like a pressed glass tile.

Lockups

The mark and the name

The wordmark is set in Sora Semibold with tight tracking. The tittle on the i is a single petal lifted from the mark, so the name carries the symbol even without the icon.

Anatomy

Five layers, taken from the reference

Each layer is a separate SVG group, so a theme can recolor or drop any of them. Small sizes keep only the first three.

1

Body gradient

Squircle (superellipse, n = 4.2). Three stops, dark at the top, bright at the base.

2

Rim light

A 3 px edge stroke, brightest at top and bottom, plus a blurred inner glow and a low sheen.

3

Glyph

Eight lens petals at 68% of the body width, filled white to a tint of the body color.

4

Bevel and shadow

A bright inner edge on every petal, a soft shade inside it, and a 7 px drop shadow.

5

Halo

The body, blurred in the rim color at 22%, so the icon glows on dark docks.

Sizes

From splash to tray

Rendered at the hicolor sizes the desktop file installs. At 32 px and below the halo, bevel and shadow drop out and the rim thickens so the edge still reads.

In context

Where people see it

The launcher's own empty library, and the app grid and dock of a desktop. The launcher UI keeps its theme accent; the icon colorway is independent.
